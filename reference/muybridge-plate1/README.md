# Muybridge, Animal Locomotion plate 1 (1887): a man walking

Frames 1-12 of the profile row, used as the reference for Matt's walk.
Public domain (Public Domain Mark), from the Wellcome Collection:
https://wellcomecollection.org/works/bgd7pfcz (IIIF image B11853098).

The 12 frames cover just under one stride, about 1/14 of a stride apart:
contact at frames 2-3, push-off 4-5, passing 6-7, contact on the other leg
9-10, push-off 11-12. Measured by eye against the grid: step (heel to heel)
about 0.41 x height; at contact the front heel is about 0.23 x height ahead of
the hip and the back toe about 0.26 behind; push-off brings the foot to about
60 deg; swing knee about 70 deg; body rise and fall about 3% of height;
forward lean of a few degrees with the head down.

`tools/compare-walk.js <page dir> reference/muybridge-plate1 <out.png>` renders
Matt at the matching moments under these frames (needs the page's debug hook,
see the script); `tools/walk-keyposes.py` makes the four key-pose pairs.
