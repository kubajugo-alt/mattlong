# Interface art (Gemini)

Painted with `tools/gen-image.mjs` (gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview; the pro model was overloaded), the office
painting as style reference, objects on chroma green and keyed with `tools/key_sprite.py`. `raw/` holds the
generations with their prompt sidecars (.json); the keyed PNGs sit here; the demo uses webp copies in
`demo/office-walk/ui/`.

- Items (Prologue): eviction notice, hip flask, Pete's cigarettes (invented "Bowery" brand), pawn ticket 4417,
  Lee Wing's account book, fifty dollars, photo of Michael, Michael's door key.
- Buttons: fedora (inventory), notebook (journal), torch (show hotspots), map, pocket watch (menu); half-mask icon
  for Mask choices.
- Panels: film strip (inventory, 8 frames at 1.35/13.79/26.16/38.51/50.79/63.07/75.38/87.75 % across), title card frame.
- Dropped: fedora-interior inventory and typewriter-paper dialogue panel (too intrusive), cigar-box tray, back of
  Natalia's photo (Gemini invented a plot clue on it).
